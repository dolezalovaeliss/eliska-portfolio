ArmaCover Logomark

Logomark for ArmaCover, consumer cyber insurance by Cybertrends that covers identity theft, scams, cyber extortion and cyberbullying.

The symbol is an A built from two folded planes, like armour plates that overlap to cover what is underneath. Sharp and steady, it pairs with a heavy wordmark and stands on its own as the app icon.
