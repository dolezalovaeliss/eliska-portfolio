Importing metadata in 3 clicks instead of 10

Importing metadata is the first real thing a customer does in Ataccama ONE, and it took 6 clicks, sometimes 10, drilling into one connection at a time with no sense of where you were. I redesigned it as a single view of the whole source, connection, schema and table tree, with an import entry point right in the Data Catalog.

Designed in Figma, validated with consultants, then rebuilt as a working code prototype. Testing removed a preview step, and the measured flow went from 6–10 clicks down to 3.
