Disciplines

Outputs
Brand Strategy Visual Identity Web Design

Dluhopisomat opens corporate bonds to retail investors, a category long reserved for institutions. The product worked, but the brand wasn’t keeping up. I led a strategy-led redesign of the identity and the homepage, built around how trust is communicated to first-time investors.

After twelve directions across three palette families, the brand landed on deep teal with a confident green accent, everyday Czech photography, and a homepage that answers the trust questions before asking for anything.
