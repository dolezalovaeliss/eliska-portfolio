KVIFF Group

Visual work for KVIFF Group, the family of brands around the Karlovy Vary International Film Festival. I worked across its year-round brands, KVIFF.TV, KVIFF Talents, Prague Shorts, Aerofilms and KVIFF Classics, and how they show up in digital and on social media.

For the festival itself I prepared printed materials together with the KVIFF team and Studio Najbrt, the studio behind the festival visual. The slides here are one piece of that work: an overview of the brands for a group presentation, each in its own logotype, typography and colour on one shared grid.
