Asset Layout Editor

Customers extend Ataccama ONE’s metadata model with custom properties, but the only way to surface them was a hidden JSON editor nobody outside engineering could use. I replaced it with a pin model: admins pick the properties that matter, and they appear at the top of every entity page of that type.

Grounded in research across 22 sources and six competitors, the layout stays locked with one clear escape hatch. No JSON, no code, delivered as a working prototype in six weeks.
