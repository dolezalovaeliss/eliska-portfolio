About me

I’m Eliška, a product & visual designer based in Prague. I help teams turn complex products into clear, calm interfaces, and give brands a visual language that holds together across web, print, social and motion.

I work with tech companies and startups, agencies and studios, and cultural organisations. Usually on product design, websites and visual identity, often all three for the same client.

I started in graphic design, doing brands and websites, and spent a lot of those years around culture, working with festivals like KVIFF and Prague Pride. Later I moved into UX, studied it during my master’s, and ended up in product design, where I get to understand the whole problem and help decide where a product should go.

I build my own AI skills and a context system in Obsidian, so the tools work the way I do and I can prototype in real code.

I’m open to new collaborations. Write me and tell me what you’re working on.
