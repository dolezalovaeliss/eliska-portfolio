Atelier3

Creative concept and website for Atelier3, an architectural design and engineering practice. The whole concept grew from one idea: thoughtful details make a complete whole. Precision, expertise and fair partnership became the backbone of the site, with large photography and generous space so the buildings can breathe.

As art director I set the visual language: black and white with a bolder signature green for courage and growth, and a clean geometric bold typeface with architecture in every stroke. No decoration, just legibility and strength.
