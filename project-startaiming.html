Aim

Website for Aim by Startaiming, a market-data product made for people and their AI agents. Think Bloomberg Terminal, but for your competitors, the people shaping your market and your prospects.

The site explains a new kind of product in a few calm steps: data organised into spaces, then you just ask. A single blue sphere carries the idea of all that context in one place, and the same clean system runs through pricing and the in-product prompts to install Aim or book a call.

Made in collaboration with ZeroOne Company, with Aleš as art director.
