ArmaCover

Brand animations for ArmaCover, consumer cyber insurance by Cybertrends. Each loop turns one promise into a few seconds of motion: a protective sphere around everyday life covering identity theft, scams, cyber extortion and cyberbullying, a dark web alert that warns you early, and recovery experts available 24/7.

Warm sand and a single orange glow carry the identity from the product UI into photography, so the protection feels calm rather than alarming.
