Cybertrends Logomark

Logomark for Cybertrends, a company building digital protection products that help people move through the online world with more confidence.

The symbol turns the C into a shield that opens into rays of light: protection that stays calm and lets people move forward. It sits in front of a bold, compact wordmark and works on its own as the app icon and favicon.
