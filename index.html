I’m a product & visual designer based in Prague, currently Head of Design at Cybertrends. I like understanding the whole problem, not just one screen, and helping decide where a product should go.

I started in graphic design, doing brands and websites, and spent a lot of those years around culture, working with festivals like KVIFF and Prague Pride. Later I moved into UX during my master’s.
