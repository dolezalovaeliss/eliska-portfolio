Product & Visual Designer

I’m a product & visual designer based in Prague. I design digital products, websites and brand visuals for tech companies, startups and cultural organisations, from the structure of a complex tool to the way a brand looks and moves.

I like understanding the whole problem, not just one screen. My work spans data and security products, healthcare software, websites and festivals like KVIFF and Prague Pride.

I’m open to new collaborations. Reach me via email, find me on LinkedIn or download my CV.
