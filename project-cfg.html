Comfort Finance Group

Comfort Finance Group is a Czech investment group spanning investing, secured products, real estate and fintech. I redesigned its website to present CFG as one confident, modern financial group, while giving each sub-brand, Comfort Money, Dluhopisomat, Srovnávač and more, a clear place in the story.

A system of deep forest green, warm cream and a single violet accent reserved for actions, set in Manrope and carried from the homepage through to the careers page.
